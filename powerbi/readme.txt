Business Intelligence Dashboards
Dashboard 1: Product Performance Dashboard
Provides insights into product revenue, units sold, product popularity, demand trends, and ABC classification for inventory optimization.

Dashboard 2: Inventory Health Dashboard
Monitors inventory turnover, stock risk levels, product aging, branch inventory distribution, and stockout risks.

Dashboard 3: Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard
Analyzes warehouse utilization, throughput, capacity gaps, branch benchmarking, and operational efficiency across warehouse locations.

Dashboard 4: Supplier Performance Dashboard
Evaluates supplier contribution, reliability, lead times, dependency risks, critical suppliers, and supplier diversification across product categories.
